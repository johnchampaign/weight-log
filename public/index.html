Custom period

Rates are a least-squares fit to the daily trend over each period, as in Hacker's Diet Online. Periods end on your most recent weigh-in.

Diet plan

Set a goal and a daily deficit. Tick "Show the plan" to draw it on the Log and Chart tabs as a dashed yellow line, which stays flat at your goal weight once the plan's end date passes, so you can compare your actual trend against it. Your height, for body mass index, is on the Settings tab.

Display is the unit you see and type everywhere on the site. Log is the unit your weights are stored and exported in; changing it between kilograms and pounds converts your stored weights. Stone logs are kept in pounds, and stones show as stones and pounds (14 4.2).
