Diet plan

Set a goal and a daily calorie deficit. Tick "Show the plan" to draw it on the Log and Chart tabs as a dashed yellow line, which stays flat at your goal weight once the plan's end date passes, so you can compare your actual trend against it.

Trend: exponentially smoothed moving average (10% smoothing), after John Walker's Hacker's Diet.
